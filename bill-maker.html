Bill vs. Invoice: Which One Should You Use?

While often used interchangeably in casual business speech, they represent two perspectives of the exact same financial transaction:

  • Invoice: Issued by the seller or contractor as a formal payment request detailing credit terms (e.g., Net 14 or Net 30 days).
  • Bill: Received and logged by the customer as an obligation to pay (Accounts Payable), often expecting immediate settlement upon delivery.
