About QuickBillFree

Empowering freelancers and independent business owners with free, private financial software.

Our Origin: Why We Built QuickBillFree

QuickBillFree was founded with a singular purpose: to end the "invoicing paywall trap."

Every independent contractor, freelancer, and gig worker has experienced the exact same frustration: you search online for a simple invoice template, spend fifteen minutes filling out client information, hourly rates, and deliverables, and click "Download PDF." Suddenly, a modal appears demanding a $30/month recurring subscription or forcing you to create an account and surrender your contact information just to receive a basic PDF file.

We believe creating and issuing a professional bill to your client is a fundamental utility that should always be 100% free, instant, and private.

Our Core Engineering Principles

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy

Unlike cloud-based SaaS portals, QuickBillFree processes all calculations, PDF rendering, and data storage exclusively inside your web browser using HTML5 LocalStorage. Your customer details, rates, revenue numbers, and logos are never sent to or stored on external servers.

⚡ Zero Sign-Up & No Login

No accounts to create, no passwords to remember, and no spam newsletters. Open the web app, customize your line items, and download print-ready vector PDFs in under 30 seconds.

📱 Offline-First Progressive Web App (PWA)

Equipped with a modern Service Worker cache, QuickBillFree works seamlessly without an internet connection. Create and print client invoices on planes, remote job sites, or on mobile.
