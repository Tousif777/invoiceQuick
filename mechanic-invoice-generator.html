What Every Auto Repair Invoice Must Include

Whether you run a multi-bay certified shop or an independent mobile repair van, clean invoices build repeat customer loyalty and protect your shop in case of warranty disputes:

  • Vehicle Identification: Year, Make, Model, VIN, and current odometer reading at time of intake.
  • Itemized Parts Breakdown: OEM/Aftermarket part numbers, descriptions, quantities, and unit prices.
  • Technician Labor: Hourly diagnostic fees and flat-rate labor times (e.g., 2.5 hrs for rotor replacement).
  • Shop Supplies & Environmental Disposal: Clear documentation of fluid recycling or disposal fees where applicable.
  • Warranty Disclosures: Explicit terms (e.g., "12-month / 12,000-mile parts & labor warranty").
