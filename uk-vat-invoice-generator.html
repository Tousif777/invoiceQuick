HMRC Legal Guidelines for Issuing a UK VAT Invoice

If your business is registered for VAT in the United Kingdom, you must issue a valid VAT invoice whenever you supply goods or services to another VAT-registered business. Failure to include mandatory HMRC fields can prevent your clients from reclaiming input VAT.

1. Mandatory Fields on a Full UK VAT Invoice

  • Document Title: Must be clearly identified as a "VAT Invoice" or "Tax Invoice".
  • Sequential Identifier: A unique invoice reference number following an unbroken numbering sequence.
  • Tax Point (Time of Supply): The date the service was completed or goods sent (if different from the invoice date).
  • Supplier Details: Full legal trading name, registered office address, and 9-digit UK VAT Registration Number (e.g., GB 384 9201 55).
  • Customer Details: Name and trading or residential address of the client.
  • Detailed Breakdown: Description, quantity, unit price excluding VAT, and the VAT rate charged per item.
  • Totals Breakdown: Subtotal before tax, total VAT amount payable in sterling (£), and total gross payable.

2. Current UK VAT Rates (2026)

Depending on what your business supplies, ensure you apply the appropriate HMRC rate:

  • Standard Rate (20%): Applies to most commercial goods and professional services.
  • Reduced Rate (5%): Applies to domestic fuel and power, child car seats, and certain home energy installations.
  • Zero Rate (0%): Most food and books, children's clothes, and public transport.

3. Banking Details for Faster Payment Settlement

To avoid check delays, British businesses rely on online bank transfers. Always provide:

  • Bank Name: (e.g. Barclays, Lloyds, NatWest, HSBC, Revolut Business)
  • Sort Code: 6-digit branch identifier (e.g. 20-00-00)
  • Account Number: 8-digit UK current account number
  • IBAN & BIC: Required if receiving cross-border euro or dollar transfers.

Frequently Asked Questions for UK Businesses

Do I need to charge VAT if I am not VAT-registered?

No. If your rolling 12-month taxable turnover is below the UK VAT threshold (£90,000), you are not required to register for VAT and should NOT charge VAT or include a VAT number. In this case, simply use our Free Standard Invoice Maker with tax set to 0%.

Can I include my Companies House registration number?

Yes. If you operate as a UK Private Limited Company (Ltd), UK law requires you to display your company registered number and registered office address. You can easily add this in the business details field.
