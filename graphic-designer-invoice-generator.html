Invoicing Best Practices for Freelance Graphic Designers

Designers frequently encounter scope creep, endless revision requests, and delayed client sign-offs. Structuring your invoice properly eliminates these headaches before they start.

1. Itemize Revision Rounds

Instead of billing one flat fee for "Website Redesign", separate your base package from additional revision rounds (e.g., "Base UI Package (Includes 2 Iterations) — $2,000" vs "Additional Revision Rounds (3 hrs @ $85/hr) — $255").

2. The Essential Copyright Transfer Clause

Never transfer design file copyrights before you get paid. Always include this standard legal note in your invoice footer:

⚖️ Copy-Paste Design Copyright Clause:
"All intellectual property, vector assets, and source design files (Figma/AI/PSD) remain the exclusive property of the designer until this invoice is settled in full. Usage rights transfer automatically upon confirmed payment receipt."

3. Bill for Milestone Deposits (50/50 Rule)

For creative work over $500, always charge a 50% non-refundable deposit upfront before creating moodboards or wireframes. Then bill the remaining 50% upon final deliverable handoff.
