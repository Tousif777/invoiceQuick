What Every Professional Handyman Invoice Must Include

Handyman tasks often combine multiple small jobs into one visit. Having a clear, itemized invoice avoids disputes and ensures you get paid for both your time and the materials you purchased out of pocket.

1. Itemized List of Repairs

Group individual tasks clearly so the customer understands what was completed (e.g. "Drywall Patching & Sanding (Living Room)" vs. "Interior Door Knob Replacement").

2. Material Receipts & Markups

Homeowners expect to reimburse you for lumber, joint compound, fasteners, or specialized hardware. Detail the materials purchased with unit costs.

3. Hourly Labor vs. Flat-Rate Pricing

Whether you charge a flat $75/hour or a fixed project fee, state it unambiguously. If there was a minimum service call fee, list it as the first line item.

4. Clear Payment Terms & Instant Links

State "Payment Due: Upon Completion" and offer instant payment options like Venmo QR, Zelle phone number, or Cash App handle.

📋 Sample Handyman Line Items:
• Drywall Patching, Sanding & Primer Spot Coat ($255.00)
• Fence Post Replacement & Gate Latch Alignment ($175.00)
• Interior Solid Core Door Hanging & Hardware ($220.00)
• Materials Reimbursement (Wood, Screws, Joint Compound) ($68.00)
• Payment Terms: Due upon completion. Cash, Check, Venmo accepted.
