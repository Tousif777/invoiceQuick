Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Invoicing Rules & Thresholds

Canadian businesses that exceed the small supplier threshold of CA$30,000 in gross revenue across four consecutive calendar quarters must register for GST/HST and charge tax on their taxable commercial sales.

1. CRA Invoicing Rules by Sale Amount

  • Sales under $30: Minimal documentation required.
  • Sales between $30 and $149.99: Must show supplier's name, invoice date, total amount paid/payable, and whether the amount includes GST/HST or the tax rate charged.
  • Sales of $150 or more: In addition to the above, the invoice must show the buyer's name, a brief description of each service/item, payment terms, and the seller's 9-digit CRA Business Number (BN) with the RT tax account suffix (e.g., 89421 9021 RT0001).

2. Canadian Sales Tax Rates by Province (2026)

Frequently Asked Questions for Canadian Businesses

Can I issue invoices in US dollars for American clients?

Yes! Many Canadian tech consultants bill international clients in USD ($). QuickBillFree lets you switch currency to USD, EUR, or GBP while displaying your Canadian business address.

Do I charge GST to international clients outside Canada?

Generally, professional services exported to clients outside Canada are considered "zero-rated" (0% GST/HST) by the CRA. You can simply set the tax rate to 0% for international clients.
