When Should You Deliver a Payment Receipt?

An invoice asks for payment, but a receipt confirms payment has been received in full. Delivering an immediate stamped receipt builds trust with clients, avoids awkward double-billing inquiries, and gives both parties a record to keep.

How to Make a Receipt in 30 Seconds with QuickBillFree:

  1. Open the Free Receipt Maker.
  2. In the top bar, set Document Type to "Receipt / Bill" (or visit Bill Maker).
  3. Set the Stamp dropdown to "Stamp: PAID" (a bold PAID watermark badge will display across the document).
  4. Enter the payment transaction ID or settlement method in the notes (e.g., "Paid via Wire Transfer on Oct 9, 2026 - Ref #TXN9928").
  5. Click "📥 Download PDF" to save the receipt. Remove the QuickBillFree footer before export if you want an unbranded PDF, then send it using your own email app.

What to put on a cash payment receipt

Enter the seller or service provider, the person who paid, a unique receipt number, the date payment was received, the items or services, and the amount. Record “Cash” as the payment method in the notes. Add a transaction reference for card or bank payments when available.

A receipt records a real payment; generating a PDF does not process or verify that payment. Set the PAID stamp only after you have confirmed receipt of the money. To request a future payment instead, use the invoice generator or read invoice vs. bill vs. receipt.

What Information Must a Legally Valid Receipt Include?

To qualify as valid audit evidence for IRS expense deductions or VAT claims, your payment receipt must state:

  • Seller / Vendor Details: Registered business trade name, physical address, email, and phone number.
  • Client / Payer Details: Name or organization of the individual who paid.
  • Receipt Number & Transaction Date: A unique receipt tracking ID and the exact date payment was received.
  • Itemized Goods or Services: Quantity, item description, and unit price for each purchased deliverable.
  • Sales Tax / VAT Breakdown: Clear separation of net amount, tax percentage, and total tax paid. Calculate exact tax figures using our free VAT Calculator.
  • Settlement Method: Clarify whether payment was settled in Cash, Check (#XXXX), Bank Wire, or Card (Last 4 digits).
  • Clear PAID Status: An explicit "PAID IN FULL" label or watermark stamp.
