When Should You Deliver a Payment Receipt?

An invoice asks for payment, but a receipt confirms payment has been received in full. Delivering an immediate stamped receipt builds trust with clients and prevents double-billing disputes.

How to Make a Receipt in 30 Seconds with QuickBillFree:

  1. Open the Invoice Maker.
  2. In the top bar, set Document Type to "Receipt / Bill".
  3. Set the Stamp dropdown to "Stamp: PAID" (a bold green/red PAID badge will appear automatically).
  4. Enter the payment transaction ID or method in the notes (e.g., "Paid via Wire Transfer on Oct 9, 2026 - Ref #TXN9928").
  5. Click "📥 Download PDF" to send the official receipt to your client!
