How to import the invoice template into Google Sheets

  1. Download the XLSX workbook using the button above. No QuickBillFree account is required for the download.
  2. Sign in to Google Drive, upload the file, and choose Open with Google Sheets. Use Save as Google Sheets if you want a native copy in your Drive.
  3. Replace the business and client details. Fill the item descriptions, quantities and prices, then enter any tax rate and amount paid.
  4. Check the formulas and page setup after import. Use the print preview to export the finished invoice as a PDF.

What the spreadsheet calculates

For example, 7.5 hours at 60 plus a fixed fee of 300 gives a subtotal of 750. At 10% tax, the total is 825. Record a payment of 200 and the balance becomes 625. These are illustrative figures; enter the agreed prices and tax treatment for your own work.

Edit the pale input cells and leave the amount and total formulas intact. Currency is a label; changing it does not convert amounts. The simple template applies one tax rate to the whole subtotal and does not handle item-specific tax exemptions.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a Google Sheets copy link?

No. This is a downloadable XLSX workbook that you can import into Google Sheets. There is no public native Google Sheets copy link.

Do I need a Google account?

Downloading the workbook does not require an account. Using Google Drive and Google Sheets requires a Google account. The browser invoice generator works without one.

What calculations are included?

Each amount is quantity multiplied by unit price. The workbook totals ten item rows, applies one tax rate to the subtotal, and subtracts an amount already paid from the total.
