Best Practices for Collecting Overdue Invoices

Most unpaid invoices aren't intentional refusals — they get buried in an inbox or wait for an accounts-payable cycle. Sending a clear, structured follow-up with the exact invoice number, amount due, and payment instructions makes it easy for your client to pay immediately.

  • Always put the invoice number in the subject line: Accounting teams search by INV-####, which speeds up approval.
  • Escalate tone gradually: Start polite at 1–7 days overdue, confirm receipt at 14 days, and only reference late fees or pausing work after 30 days.
  • Attach or link the PDF again: Never make the client hunt for the original email. Need a fresh PDF? Re-open or export it from the QuickBillFree Invoice Tracker.
