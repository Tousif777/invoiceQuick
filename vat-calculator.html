How to Calculate VAT (Formulas & Explanations)

Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax assessed on the value added to goods and services. Calculating VAT accurately ensures your invoices comply with HMRC and international tax authority rules.

1. Adding VAT Formula (Net to Gross)

When you have a base price and need to add 20% VAT:

VAT Amount = Net Price × (VAT Rate ÷ 100)
Gross Total = Net Price + VAT Amount (or Net Price × 1.20)

Example: For a £500 freelance service with 20% VAT: £500 × 0.20 = £100 VAT. Gross Total = £600.

2. Removing VAT Formula (Gross to Net / Reverse VAT)

When an item costs £120 including VAT, many people mistakenly calculate 20% of £120 (£24) and subtract it. That is incorrect! The 20% was applied to the original net price, not the gross total.

Net Price = Gross Price ÷ (1 + (VAT Rate ÷ 100))
VAT Amount = Gross Price - Net Price

Correct Example: To remove 20% VAT from £120: £120 ÷ 1.20 = £100 Net. VAT Amount = £20 (which is 20% of £100).
