Choosing the Right Proposal Document for New Clients

Service contractors often use the words estimate, quote, and bid interchangeably. However, conflating them can lead to devastating contractual losses if unexpected project costs arise.

Comparison: Estimate vs. Quote vs. Bid

Attribute Estimate Quote (Quotation) Formal Bid
Binding Status Non-binding rough cost Legally binding upon acceptance Strictly binding competitive submission
Used For Early budget scoping Freelance projects & trade jobs Government & commercial tenders
Cost Flexibility Can fluctuate ±15-20% Fixed locked price Fixed sealed bid

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an estimate legally binding?

No, an estimate is a preliminary non-binding calculation that can increase if unseen complications arise.

Is a quote legally binding?

Yes! Once accepted by the client within the stated validity window, a quotation becomes a legally binding contract.
