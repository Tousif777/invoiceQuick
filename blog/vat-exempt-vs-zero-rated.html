Why VAT-Exempt and Zero-Rated Are NOT the Same

Both zero-rated and exempt goods charge 0% VAT to the customer at the checkout. However, on your VAT tax return, the accounting implications are completely opposite.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a business claim input VAT on zero-rated sales?

Yes! On zero-rated goods (0% VAT), businesses CAN claim back input tax incurred on business purchases. On VAT-exempt goods, you CANNOT reclaim input VAT.

What are examples of zero-rated goods?

In the UK and EU, zero-rated goods often include books, children's clothing, physical newspapers, and basic grocery foods.

What are examples of VAT-exempt supplies?

Financial services, life insurance, medical care, education, and residential property rentals are typically VAT-exempt.
