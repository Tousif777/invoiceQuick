Protecting Your Creative Copyright on Client Invoices

Photography is intellectual property. Delivering photos without a clear commercial usage clause allows clients to resell, syndicate, or broadcast your images worldwide without compensation.

Use our free Photography Invoice Generator to bill for shoots, or issue paper slips on location via our Blank Invoice PDF.

4 Line Items Every Commercial Photo Bill Must Have

  • Creative Day Rate: Shoot time (Half-Day 4hrs or Full-Day 8hrs).
  • Commercial Licensing Scope: Explicitly states duration (1 Year), territory (North America), and media (Digital/Social Media only).
  • Production Expenses: Lighting rental, studio space booking, digital tech fees, and makeup artists.
  • Post-Production / Retouching: Number of final high-resolution retouched assets delivered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should photographers separate creative fees from licensing fees?

The creative fee covers shooting time and labor. The licensing fee compensates you for how the client uses the images (social media, billboards, global broadcast).

What are standard photography cancellation terms?

Standard contracts retain the non-refundable 50% deposit for cancellations within 72 hours of the scheduled shoot.
