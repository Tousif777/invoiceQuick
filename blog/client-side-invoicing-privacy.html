Data Security & Privacy

How Browser-Based Invoice Storage Works

MT
MD Tousif
Published Oct 8, 2026 • Updated Nov 15, 2026 • 3 min read

QuickBillFree is a browser-based invoice generator: its editor calculates totals and builds PDFs on your device. You can make an invoice without signing up. The app saves drafts in this browser rather than offering an account with cloud synchronization.

Where are my invoice drafts saved?

The editor uses localStorage for invoice drafts and saved client details. Return with the same browser and profile to resume your work, provided storage is available and has not been cleared. A different device or browser will not automatically have those drafts.

Browser storage is convenient, but it is not an encrypted vault or a backup service. Other people using the same browser profile may see saved invoices. Clearing site data, using a private browsing session, or a storage error can remove or prevent saved drafts.

Does local invoice storage mean there is no tracking?

No. QuickBillFree loads Google Analytics, advertising and other external resources. Keeping invoice drafts in the browser is different from running a website without third-party services. See the privacy policy for the site's published data and advertising information.

Choose a tool that meets your client's data-handling requirements. Local storage alone does not establish compliance with a contract or a privacy law.

What happens when I share an invoice link?

The Share feature encodes invoice content in the URL fragment, the part after #inv=. Anyone who receives the complete link can open its invoice contents. Encoding is not encryption, and the link is not protected by a login.

Send a shared invoice link only to the intended recipient. Do not post links containing client details, payment instructions or private project information in public. Sending a PDF or an email also shares the information you include with its recipients.

How do I back up an invoice?

  1. Check the business details, recipient, items, tax and total in the editor.
  2. Choose Download PDF or Print and save a copy to a location you control.
  3. Confirm that the PDF opens correctly before clearing browser data or changing devices.

A PDF preserves the exported document; it does not provide automatic editing or synchronization of the browser draft. If you use a shared computer, keep your own copy and remove sensitive local drafts when you finish.

Use the freelance invoice generator for hours and project fees, the contractor invoice generator for labor and materials, or the blank printable invoice form for handwriting.
