The Power of Predictable Recurring Revenue

Freelancers and agencies that rely strictly on one-off project gigs live on an exhausting feast-or-famine cycle. Structuring ongoing client relationships into monthly retainer agreements transforms cash flow.

Use our Online Bill Maker or Free Invoice Generator to generate monthly billing statements effortlessly.

3 Rules for Successful Retainer Invoicing

  • Bill on the Same Day Monthly: Standardize your billing cycle to the 1st or 15th of the month.
  • Include Rollover Disclaimers: Explicitly state in notes whether unused hours expire at month's end (recommended) or roll over.
  • Embed Scannable Payment Links: Allow clients to pay in 5 seconds via QR codes or direct ACH wire transfer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a recurring invoice and a one-time invoice?

A one-time invoice bills for an isolated deliverable. A recurring invoice is automatically issued on a set schedule (e.g., 1st of every month) for ongoing services.

Should retainers be billed in advance or in arrears?

Retainers should always be billed in advance (e.g. billed on the 1st for the upcoming month's advisory services).
