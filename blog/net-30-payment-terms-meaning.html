What Does Net 30 Mean on an Invoice? Rules, Calculation & Pros/Cons

Understanding Commercial Credit Terms

When reviewing commercial bills, you will routinely encounter terms like "Net 30", "Net 15", or "2/10 Net 30". These standard payment terms dictate the credit window extended by the service provider to the purchasing party.

What is Net 30?

Net 30 means that the buyer must settle the full balance of the invoice within 30 calendar days following the date the invoice was officially issued. For example, if an invoice is dated October 1st, payment must clear in the seller's account by October 31st.

Common Variations of Net Terms

Payment Term Payment Deadline Best Used For
Due upon Receipt Immediate payment upon delivery One-off freelance work, repairs, spot retail
Net 14 / Net 15 14 to 15 calendar days Independent contractors, digital agencies
Net 30 30 calendar days Corporate B2B clients, enterprise procurement
Net 60 / Net 90 60 to 90 calendar days Government tenders, massive retail supply chains
2/10 Net 30 2% discount if paid in 10 days; else 30 days Incentivizing fast payment while preserving margin

Advantages of Offering Net 30 Terms

  • Wins Enterprise Clients: Fortune 500 companies and large institutions operate strict 30-day payment batch cycles and will refuse vendors who demand instant payment.
  • Competitive Advantage: Extending reasonable trade credit makes doing business with you frictionless compared to rigid competitors.

Risks of Net 30 for Small Businesses

  • Cash Flow Gaps: You must cover software subscriptions, payroll, and personal living costs for 30 days out-of-pocket before receiving payment.
  • High Default Risk: The longer the payment window, the greater the likelihood of invoices being misplaced in accounting departments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Net 30 mean 30 business days or 30 calendar days?

Net 30 refers to 30 calendar days from the invoice issue date, including weekends and public holidays.

What happens if a client does not pay within 30 days?

Once day 31 arrives, the invoice is legally classified as overdue. You can apply contractual late interest fees and pause ongoing project deliverables.

Should freelancers offer Net 30 terms?

For small projects under $2,000, freelancers should prefer Due upon Receipt or Net 14 to maintain healthy cash flow and avoid acting as a bank for clients.
