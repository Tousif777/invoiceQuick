CRA Compliant Invoice Requirements: Canada GST/HST Rules (2026)

MT
MD Tousif
Published Nov 15, 2026 • 6 min read

If you operate a business, subcontract, or freelance in Canada, creating a CRA compliant invoice is critical. Under Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) guidelines, if your invoice lacks specific mandatory disclosures, your commercial clients cannot claim their Input Tax Credits (ITCs) — which often leads to withheld payments and audit disputes.

Whether you charge 5% GST, 13% or 15% HST, or zero-rated exports, this guide covers exactly what the CRA requires on every invoice based on transaction size, your 9-digit Business Number (BN), and provincial tax rules.

The 3 CRA Invoice Threshold Tiers

The CRA categorizes documentary requirements for supporting Input Tax Credits into three distinct monetary tiers:

Transaction Tier Total Amount Mandatory CRA Requirements
Tier 1: Minor Purchases Under $30.00 Supplier’s business name, transaction date, and total amount paid or payable.
Tier 2: Mid-Range $30.00 to $149.99 Tier 1 info + Supplier's 9-digit Business Number (GST/HST account number), total GST/HST amount or clear indication that total includes tax.
Tier 3: Full Invoices $150.00 and over Tier 2 info + Buyer's legal or trading name, brief description of each good/service, terms of payment, and explicit breakdown of GST/HST charged.

Mandatory Checklist for Canadian Invoices ($150+)

To ensure your invoice satisfies CRA audit standards for transactions over $150, always include the following 8 items:

  1. Supplier Information: Your registered business name (or sole proprietorship legal name) and contact address.
  2. Customer Name: The recipient’s business name or trading name. (A generic "Cash Customer" invoice will disqualify the client from claiming ITCs).
  3. Invoice Date: The day the invoice is issued.
  4. CRA Business Number (GST/HST Account): Your 9-digit Business Number followed by the program identifier RT0001 (e.g., 123456789 RT0001).
  5. Description of Goods or Services: Clear itemization of labor, hours, products, or consulting milestones provided.
  6. Terms of Payment: Explicit due dates (e.g., "Net 30" or "Due upon receipt").
  7. Tax Breakdown: Clear separation between subtotal, GST (5%), PST/QST (if applicable), or combined HST (13% or 15%).
  8. Total Amount Payable: In Canadian Dollars (CAD) or foreign currency with explicit conversion indicator.
⚠️ Warning: Never Charge GST/HST Without a Registered Business Number
It is illegal under the Canadian Excise Tax Act to collect GST or HST from customers if you do not possess an active GST/HST account with the CRA. If you are a "Small Supplier" earning under $30,000 annually and have not registered, you must not show tax line items on your invoice.

Current Canadian GST, HST & Provincial Tax Rates (2026)

Canadian sales tax depends on the place of supply (where the service or product is delivered):

Province / Territory Tax Type Total Tax Rate
Ontario HST 13%
Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland & PEI HST 15%
Alberta, Northwest Territories, Nunavut, Yukon GST only 5%
British Columbia GST (5%) + PST (7%) 12%
Quebec GST (5%) + QST (9.975%) 14.975%
