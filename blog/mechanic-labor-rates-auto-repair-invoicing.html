Auto Mechanic Invoicing: Labor Rates, Parts Markup & Disclosures

MT
MD Tousif
Published Nov 15, 2026 • 9 min read

Professional Billing for Independent Auto Shops & Mobile Mechanics

Automotive repair invoicing is subject to strict state consumer protection statutes (such as California's Bureau of Automotive Repair). Failing to detail parts, labor, odometer readings, and warranty disclosures can expose shop owners to disputes.

Use our dedicated Auto Mechanic Invoice Generator to generate compliant repair orders and final billing receipts.

Key Elements Every Automotive Bill Must Detail:

  1. Vehicle Identification Data: Record the 17-character VIN (Vehicle Identification Number), exact odometer reading upon entry, and license plate number.
  2. Itemized Parts Breakdown: Clearly state whether replacement components are OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer), aftermarket new, rebuilt, or salvaged.
  3. Labor Classification: Document the agreed hourly rate (e.g. $125/hr) multiplied by authorized labor guide hours.
  4. Environmental & Shop Fees: Clearly separate tire disposal, hazardous waste, and shop supply surcharges.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information must an auto repair invoice legally contain?

State automotive repair bureau laws require: shop license number, customer name, vehicle VIN, year/make/model, odometer mileage, separation of parts/labor, and warranty statements.

What is a shop supplies fee on a mechanic bill?

A standard 5% to 10% fee (often capped at $35-$50) covering non-itemized consumables like brake cleaner, rags, solvents, and environmental disposal.

What is book time in automotive repair billing?

Book time uses industry labor guides (like Mitchell or AllData) to bill a standardized number of hours for a specific repair regardless of how fast the technician completes it.
