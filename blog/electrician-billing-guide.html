Professional Invoicing for Master & Journeyman Electricians

Electrical contracting requires rigorous compliance with the National Electrical Code (NEC). Homeowners and general contractors need clear documentation showing that work was executed safely and permitted properly.

Mandatory Inclusions for Electrical Billing

  1. Master License & Bond Details: Display your state license number (e.g. Lic #EC-98412) to establish legal compliance.
  2. City & County Permit Surcharges: List municipal permit and inspector sign-off fees separately from billable labor.
  3. Materials Itemization: Detail copper wire gauges (12/2, 10/3, 6/3), subpanels, breakers, GFCI outlets, and conduit lengths.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard hourly rate for an electrician?

Journeyman and master electricians bill between $75 and $150 per hour, depending on state licensing and local union scales.

Must an electrician invoice include license numbers?

Yes. Most state electrical boards legally mandate that your master electrician license number be prominently displayed on all estimates and bills.

How should electricians bill for EV charger installations?

Itemize the 240V breaker, dedicated Romex wire run per foot, wall-mounted charger hardware, and municipal city permit inspection fees.
