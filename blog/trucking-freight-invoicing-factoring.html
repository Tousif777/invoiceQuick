Crucial Data Fields on a Carrier Freight Invoice

  1. Load & Rate Con Reference: State the broker's Load ID and Master Waybill Number.
  2. Origin & Destination: List exact shipper and consignee warehouse addresses.
  3. Mileage & Base Linehaul Rate: Miles traveled multiplied by the agreed per-mile rate.
  4. Accessorial Charges: Detention fees, lumper fees, stop-off charges, and FSC (Fuel Surcharge).

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents must be attached to a trucking invoice?

Carriers must attach the signed Proof of Delivery (POD) / Bill of Lading (BOL) and the signed Rate Confirmation sheet to get paid.

How do owner-operators bill for driver detention?

Detention is billed hourly (typically $50–$100/hr) for delays exceeding the standard 2-hour free loading/unloading window.
