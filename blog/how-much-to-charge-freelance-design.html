Designers frequently suffer from "scope creep"—where a simple client logo request balloons into endless color tweaks and collateral revisions. Setting clear billing rules and professional invoices protects your creative business.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average hourly rate for a freelance graphic designer in 2026?

Junior designers average $40–$60/hr, mid-level designers bill $65–$95/hr, and senior brand strategists bill $100–$175+/hr.

Should designers charge flat rates or hourly?

Package defined assets (logos, pitch decks, style guides) into flat fees with strict revision caps (e.g. 2 rounds included). Bill scope creep at your hourly rate.
