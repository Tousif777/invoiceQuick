Protecting Your Business During Cash Transactions

Cash payments provide immediate liquidity, but without written proof, cash transactions are a magnet for client disputes and tax audit penalties.

Issue instant stamped digital receipts with our Online Receipt Maker, or print physical forms for job-site handwriting via our Printable Blank Invoice PDF.

Essential Fields on a Valid Cash Receipt

  1. Explicit 'PAID IN CASH' Stamp: Clear indication that physical legal tender was settled.
  2. Date & Exact Currency Amount: The precise calendar day funds were handed over.
  3. Vendor Name & Tax ID: Establishes who collected the cash for taxable income records.
  4. Sequential Receipt Number: Prevents missing numbers in internal auditing books.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a business legally required to provide a receipt for cash?

Yes, in nearly all commercial jurisdictions, customers have a legal right to request a written or digital receipt for any cash transaction.

What is the IRS Form 8300 rule for cash payments?

Businesses must file IRS Form 8300 if they receive more than $10,000 in cash from one buyer in a single transaction or related transactions.
