Legal Invoicing Standards Under UK Law

If you operate as a self-employed sole trader or independent freelancer in England, Scotland, Wales, or Northern Ireland, Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC) enforces specific legal standards for all client invoices.

Failing to include mandatory details can lead to your clients' accounting teams rejecting your bills or delays during HMRC tax audits. Use our dedicated UK VAT Invoice Generator or calculate reverse taxes with our VAT Calculator.

Mandatory Checklist for Sole Trader Invoices (Non-VAT Registered)

If your annual turnover is below the £90,000 threshold and you are not registered for VAT, your invoices must contain:

  1. Your Legal Name & Trading Name: You must state your real personal name (e.g., John Doe) and your trading business name (e.g., Doe Electrical Services).
  2. Your UK Contact Address: The physical business address where legal documents can be served.
  3. Customer Name & Address: The formal recipient business name and property location.
  4. Unique Sequential Invoice Number: A sequential alphanumeric tracker (e.g. INV-2026-001).
  5. Date of Supply (Tax Point): The date work was completed and the invoice issue date.
  6. Clear Service Breakdown: Quantities, hourly rates, and total payable amount.

Additional Requirements for VAT-Registered Sole Traders

If your business is VAT registered, your document becomes a legal VAT Invoice and must also display:

  • Your 9-digit UK VAT Registration Number (e.g., GB 123 4567 89).
  • The net price of goods before VAT.
  • The VAT rate applied per line item (Standard 20%, Reduced 5%, or Zero 0%).
  • The total VAT amount payable in British Pounds (£).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do UK sole traders have to register for VAT?

Only if your taxable turnover exceeds the UK VAT registration threshold (£90,000 as of 2024/2026). Below this threshold, you can issue non-VAT invoices.

What legal name must appear on a UK sole trader invoice?

You must display your personal name (e.g., Jane Smith) and your business trading name (e.g., Apex Design) if you trade under a brand.

What is the standard VAT rate in the UK?

The UK standard VAT rate is 20%. Reduced rate is 5%, and zero-rated items include books, children's clothes, and most food.
