How to Write a Payment Reminder Email: 5 Tested Copy-Paste Templates

MT
MD Tousif
Published Nov 15, 2026 • 8 min read

Stop Chasing Clients: The Science of Effective Follow-Up

Unpaid invoices are the #1 threat to freelance and small business cash flow. Over 48% of commercial invoices are settled past their agreed due date. The key to getting paid promptly without burning bridges is sending structured, professional follow-up emails at specific intervals.

Pair your reminders with accurate interest calculations using our free Late Fee Calculator, or review our guide on Invoice Payment Terms.

The 4-Stage Payment Reminder Timeline

  1. Stage 1 (3 Days Before Due Date): A friendly check-in to confirm the invoice was received and queued in accounts payable.
  2. Stage 2 (On Due Date): A direct notification confirming payment is due today with direct bank wire or payment links.
  3. Stage 3 (7 Days Past Due): A firm reminder highlighting outstanding days and requesting a payment status update.
  4. Stage 4 (14+ Days Past Due): A formal overdue notice notifying the client of accrued late payment interest.

Template 1: Friendly Pre-Due Date Check-In (3 Days Prior)

Subject: Friendly Reminder: Invoice [Invoice #] due on [Due Date] - [Your Company Name]

Hi [Client First Name],

I hope you're having a productive week! This is a brief note to remind you that Invoice [Invoice #] for $[Total Amount] is scheduled for payment on [Due Date].

For your convenience, I have attached a copy of the invoice PDF. You can also settle payment directly via [Payment Method: ACH / Stripe / PayPal].

Please let me know if you need any additional documentation to process remittance.

Warm regards,
[Your Name]

Template 2: On the Due Date

Subject: Invoice [Invoice #] is Due Today - [Your Company Name]

Hi [Client First Name],

I hope you're doing well. Just a quick reminder that Invoice [Invoice #] ($[Total Amount]) is due today, [Due Date].

Please confirm once the payment transfer has been submitted so I can mark your account as settled and send over your receipt.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

Template 3: 7 Days Past Due (Firm Follow-Up)

Subject: Past Due: Invoice [Invoice #] - 7 Days Overdue

Hi [Client First Name],

I am following up regarding Invoice [Invoice #] for $[Total Amount], which was due on [Due Date] and is now 7 days past due.

Could you please provide an estimated payment date or let me know if there are any questions regarding the deliverables?

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Template 4: 14+ Days Past Due (Late Fees Applied)

Subject: Second Notice: Invoice [Invoice #] is Overdue (Interest Applied)

Dear [Client First Name],

Our records indicate that Invoice [Invoice #] ($[Total Amount]) remains unpaid despite multiple follow-up notices.

In accordance with our agreed payment terms, a statutory late fee of $[Late Fee Amount] has been assessed to your balance. The new total due is $[New Total].

Please settle the outstanding balance immediately via the payment coordinates on the attached updated invoice.

Regards,
[Your Name]

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I send the first payment reminder email?

Send a friendly reminder 3 to 5 business days BEFORE the invoice due date to confirm receipt and verify payment processing schedules.

Can I charge late fees on past-due invoices?

Yes, provided a late payment clause (such as 1.5% monthly interest) was specified on the original invoice or contract agreement.

What should the email subject line say?

Keep it clear and specific: e.g. 'Reminder: Invoice INV-1042 due on Nov 20 - QuickBillFree' or 'Overdue Notice: Invoice INV-1042 past due'.
