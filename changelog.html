v1.2.0 — Invoice Tracker Dashboard & Payment Reminder Generator

November 2026

Two new free client-side tools launched to help freelancers and small businesses get paid faster.

  • Invoice Tracker Dashboard — Track Paid, Sent / Pending, Overdue, and Drafts in one dashboard. Syncs directly with your saved invoices in browser localStorage. Includes status filter pills, search, Open / Duplicate / → Receipt / Remind actions, status switching, and JSON Backup / Restore.
