What is the Difference: Quote vs. Estimate vs. Invoice?

Before sending price details to a potential client, understanding the legal differences between these three terms is crucial:

Document Type Legal Status Price Flexibility When Issued
Estimate Non-binding guide Flexible (costs may change) During initial project scoping
Quotation (Quote) Legally binding once signed Fixed agreed rate Before work starts (valid 30 days)
Invoice Commercial demand for payment Fixed debt outstanding After milestone delivery or completion

5 Essential Elements of a Winning Quotation

  1. Comprehensive Scope Breakdown: Detail distinct project phases rather than a single lump sum (e.g., Discovery, UX Design, Coding, Deployment).
  2. Clear Expiration Date: Always include an explicit validity window (14 or 30 days) to prevent clients from locking in outdated pricing months later.
  3. Deposit & Milestone Schedule: Specify upfront retainer requirements (e.g., "30% deposit required upon quote approval").
  4. Scope Change Clause: Note that additional revisions or deliverables beyond the agreed scope will be billed at your standard hourly rate (calculate yours with our Hourly Rate Calculator).
  5. Signature & Acceptance Line: Leave space for the client's authorized manager to physically or digitally sign the quote.
