What is a Proforma Invoice and When is it Used?

A Proforma Invoice is a preliminary bill of sale sent to buyers in advance of a shipment or deliverable handoff. It commits the seller to deliver the specified goods or services at the agreed prices and dates.

Key Differences: Proforma Invoice vs Commercial Invoice

  • Proforma Invoice: Sent before manufacturing or dispatch. It is not an accounting demand for payment and cannot be recorded in accounts payable ledgers.
  • Commercial Invoice: Sent after goods are shipped or work is done. It is the final tax document requesting actual payment.
