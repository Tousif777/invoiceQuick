How the Client-Side Invoice Tracker Works

Every invoice you edit or export in the QuickBillFree Invoice Generator is automatically synced to your browser's local storage. This dashboard reads that same saved history so you can manage your billing pipeline without creating an account or uploading client data to a third-party cloud.

  • Track payment status: Switch any invoice between Draft, Sent, Paid, and Overdue. Marking an invoice Paid also updates its stamp in the editor.
  • 1-click repeat billing: Click Duplicate on last month's invoice to clone the client and line items with an auto-incremented invoice number and today's date.
  • Quote → Invoice → Receipt workflow: Click → Receipt to turn a paid invoice into a payment receipt PDF, or click Remind on an unpaid bill to open the Overdue Payment Reminder Generator pre-filled with that invoice's details.
  • Back up anytime: Because browser storage lives on your device, use Backup JSON regularly to save a portable copy of your invoice history.
