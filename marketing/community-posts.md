## First-invoice post

Candidate venue: r/SideProject, subject to current rules and account eligibility.

**Title:** I built a free invoice maker for freelancers who need a PDF without creating an account

**Body:**

Disclosure: I built QuickBillFree, a free browser-based invoice generator.

When preparing an invoice, I use this checklist:

1. Give it a unique reference number.
2. Identify the business issuing it and the client being billed.
3. Describe the work clearly, with hours, quantity or fixed project fees.
4. Show the issue date and the due date agreed with the client.
5. Check the subtotal, any applicable tax, amount already paid and balance due.
6. Include the agreed payment instructions and any project reference the client needs.
7. Export a copy and review it before sending.

For illustration: 4 hours at 50 plus a fixed fee of 75 gives a subtotal of 275. With no tax in this example and 100 already received, the balance due is 175. Use your actual currency and applicable requirements.

QuickBillFree lets you edit those details and generate a PDF without signing up. Drafts are stored in the browser; keep exported copies because there is no automatic account sync. The website uses ads and analytics. It does not collect or verify payments.

## Follow-up topic: agreed deposits

Use only after checking the venue's rules, and do not post multiple promotional drafts into the same community in quick succession.

**Title:** How I show a deposit and remaining balance on a freelance invoice

**Body:**

Disclosure: I built QuickBillFree.

A deposit percentage should follow the agreement with the client. For a simple illustration, a project total of 500 with 200 already received leaves 300 due. The invoice should make all three amounts clear. A receipt can acknowledge the 200 payment while the invoice still has a remaining balance.

In the tool I built, you can enter the total and amount paid before exporting a PDF. Recording a payment in the tool does not confirm it reached your bank account.

Example tool: https://quickbillfree.com/freelance-invoice-generator.html?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community_post&utm_campaign=first_invoice&utm_content=agreed_deposit

## Follow-up topic: payment dates

**Title:** One small invoice detail I check: a clear due date

**Body:**

Disclosure: I built QuickBillFree.

Before sending an invoice, check that the due date matches the terms agreed with the client. If the agreement is payment 14 days after issue, show a specific calendar date as well as the term. Fourteen days is an example, not a universal best term.

Confirm the client's billing contact and any purchase-order reference before sending. Do not add an unexpected late-payment charge to create urgency.
