# 5-Freelancer Usability Trial Protocol & Log

> **Objective:** Have 5 real freelancers test QuickBillFree by completing an invoice.  
> **Goal:** Observe where they hesitate, verify if they reach `download_invoice_pdf`, and capture qualitative feedback on missing fields or confusing steps.

---

## 📋 The Tester Prompt (What to ask them)

Send this exact message to 5 freelancer contacts, colleagues, or community members:

```text
Hey [Name],

I built a free browser-based invoice tool called QuickBillFree for freelancers who just want a clean PDF without creating an account or paying a subscription.

Could you do me a quick 2-minute favor?
1. Open this link: https://quickbillfree.com/freelance-invoice-generator.html?utm_source=user_trial&utm_medium=usability_test&utm_campaign=first_invoice
2. Try creating a quick test invoice (e.g., 10 hours @ $50/hr, add a $100 deposit paid, pick a due date).
3. Download the PDF.

Tell me honestly:
• Did anything feel clunky or confusing while editing?
• Did the downloaded PDF look how you expected?
• Is there any field you normally need that is missing?
```

## 🔍 Key Friction Points to Watch For
1. **Adding/Editing Line Items:** Do they easily find how to enter hours vs fixed price?
2. **Deposit / Amount Paid:** Do they understand where to record money already received?
3. **Due Dates & Terms:** Do they understand the Net 7 / Net 14 / Net 30 buttons?
4. **PDF Output Quality:** Does the downloaded PDF layout look balanced and professional on their device?
