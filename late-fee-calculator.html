Understanding Statutory Late Payment Legislation & Rights

When clients fail to pay invoices on time, small business cash flow suffers immediately. Fortunately, in many jurisdictions (including the UK, EU, and under US commercial contract law), creditors have statutory legal rights to charge interest and debt recovery costs.

1. Daily Interest Calculation Formula

Interest on unpaid commercial debts accumulates daily from the day the bill becomes overdue until paid in full:

Daily Interest = (Debt Amount × Annual Interest Rate) ÷ 365
Total Interest = Daily Interest × Number of Days Overdue

Example: An unpaid $5,000 invoice that is 60 days late at 10% annual interest:
($5,000 × 0.10) ÷ 365 = $1.369 per day.
For 60 days: 60 × $1.369 = $82.19 in accrued interest.

2. Fixed Debt Recovery Compensation Fees (Tiered)

Under the UK Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 and the European Late Payment Directive (2011/7/EU), you are entitled to claim a reasonable fixed compensation fee for each overdue invoice:

Unpaid Invoice Size Statutory Compensation Fee
Under £1,000 / $1,000 £40.00 / $40.00
£1,000 to £9,999.99 £70.00 / $70.00
£10,000 or more £100.00 / $100.00

3. Steps to Collect Unpaid Invoices Professionally

  1. Friendly Reminder (Day 1–7): Send a polite email confirming the original invoice was received and checking if there were any questions.
  2. Formal Statement of Account (Day 14): Send a statement showing the overdue status and offering instant payment links.
  3. Final Notice with Statutory Interest (Day 30+): Issue an updated invoice including the statutory interest and debt recovery fee calculated above. This creates legal urgency without immediate litigation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I charge late fees to consumer clients?

Commercial debt laws primarily protect business-to-business (B2B) transactions. For individual consumer clients (B2C), you can only charge late fees if they were clearly stated and agreed to in your original signed contract or terms of service prior to starting work.
