When Do You Legally Need to Issue a Tax Invoice?

A standard invoice requests payment, but a Tax Invoice is an official government-regulated document required whenever a VAT or GST registered business sells goods or services to another registered entity.

Mandatory Information Required on a Tax Invoice:

  • Title: The document must prominently state "Tax Invoice".
  • Seller’s Tax ID: VAT Number (EU/UK), GSTIN (India), ABN (Australia), or EIN/Tax ID (USA).
  • Buyer’s Tax ID: Needed if the buyer claims input tax credits on business returns.
  • Tax Breakdown: Explicit line stating the exact tax percentage applied and dollar amount calculated.
  • Sequential Invoice Number & Date: Required for government auditing trails.
