How to use the Excel invoice template

  1. Open the downloaded XLSX file in Excel. Replace the business and client placeholders, then enter the invoice number, dates and currency.
  2. Enter a description, quantity and unit price on each item row. For hourly work, use hours as the quantity. For a fixed fee, use quantity 1.
  3. Set the tax rate and amount already paid. Formula cells calculate the subtotal, tax, total and remaining balance.
  4. Check every amount, then print or save as PDF. The workbook has a one-page A4 print area; preview it before sending.

What the spreadsheet calculates

For example, 7.5 hours at 60 plus a fixed fee of 300 gives a subtotal of 750. At 10% tax, the total is 825. Record a payment of 200 and the balance becomes 625. These are illustrative figures; enter the agreed prices and tax treatment for your own work.

Edit the pale input cells and leave the amount and total formulas intact. Currency is a label; changing it does not convert amounts. The simple template applies one tax rate to the whole subtotal and does not handle item-specific tax exemptions.
