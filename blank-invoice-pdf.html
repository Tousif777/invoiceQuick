How to fill in the printable invoice

  1. Download the matching paper size and print at actual size. Check the preview before printing multiple copies.
  2. Add your business contact details, client, a unique invoice number, issue date, agreed due date and currency.
  3. Describe each service or product. Multiply its quantity or hours by its rate to get the line amount.
  4. Add the line amounts for the subtotal, then any applicable tax to get the total.
  5. Subtract money already received from the total to show the balance due. Add payment instructions and keep a copy.

Worked example: labor, materials and a deposit

For illustration: 4 hours × 50 = 200 for labor, plus 75 for materials gives a subtotal of 275. With no tax in this example and 100 already paid, the balance due is 175. Use the currency and tax treatment applicable to your transaction.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a direct PDF download?

Yes. The A4 and US Letter links download a ready-made, one-page PDF without opening the editor or asking you to register.

Can I type into this PDF?

These PDFs are printable forms, not interactive fillable PDFs. To type your details and calculate totals, use the online invoice editor. For an editable document, use the Word or Google Docs template.

Which paper size should I choose?

Choose A4 for 210 × 297 mm paper, or US Letter for 8.5 × 11 inch paper. Print at actual size on matching paper; use Fit if your printer needs larger margins.

Does the blank PDF calculate tax or totals?

No. Calculate the six line amounts, subtotal, applicable tax, total and balance yourself. The online editor and Excel workbook provide calculated totals.

Is the form branded or watermarked?

No. The printed form contains no QuickBillFree logo, promotion or watermark.

Can I record a deposit?

Yes. Enter the full total, the amount already paid and the remaining balance due. Confirm payment before recording an amount as paid.
