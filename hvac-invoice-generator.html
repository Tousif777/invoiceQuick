Professional Billing Guide for HVAC Contractors

Heating and cooling service calls often involve high-ticket equipment, expensive refrigerant, and warranty obligations. A structured, transparent invoice protects your cash flow and builds immediate customer trust.

1. Document Equipment Make, Model & Serial Number

Always list the condenser or furnace make, model, and serial number in the notes. This provides vital verification for manufacturer warranty coverage and parts returns.

2. Separate Diagnostic Fees from Repair Labor

Customers appreciate clarity. State your initial inspection or diagnostic fee clearly, followed by approved labor and specific component replacements.

3. Detail Refrigerant Recovery & Environmental Compliance

When reclaiming or charging refrigerant, list the exact pounds supplied. Mention your EPA Section 608 compliance to demonstrate professional certified standards.

4. Fast Payment Terms (Check, Card, Zelle)

Residential HVAC repairs are typically due upon completion. Add your payment options or a payment QR code directly onto your PDF invoice.

📋 Sample HVAC Service Line Items:
• Residential AC Diagnostic & Electrical Inspection ($125.00)
• 45/5 MFD Dual Run Round Capacitor OEM Replacement ($165.00)
• R-410A Refrigerant System Recharge (3 lbs @ $75.00/lb)
• Condenser Coil Chemical Wash & Debris Clearing ($140.00)
• Terms: Due on completion. 1-year warranty on installed parts.
