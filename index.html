Accounting Standard

Essential Information on a Legal Invoice

An invoice is a formal commercial instrument. In order for client accounting departments to process payment without delays, include these itemized elements:

✓
Your Full Contact Info: Registered business name, address, phone number & email.
✓
Client Business Details: Legal company name, billing department & contact person.
✓
Sequential Invoice ID: Unique alphanumeric tracker (e.g. INV-1001) for tax records.
✓
Clear Dates: Both the Date of Issue and agreed Payment Due Date.
✓
Itemized Line Items: Specific service deliverables, hourly rates, and applicable taxes.
⏳
Payment Deadlines

Understanding Invoice Payment Terms

Precise payment terms establish mutual clarity, protect your cash flow, and prevent awkward follow-ups:

Due on Receipt
Immediate payment upon invoice delivery. Best for small one-off freelance deliverables.
Net 15 Recommended
Payment due within 15 calendar days. The modern standard for independent contractors.
Net 30
Standard corporate enterprise terms giving accounting 30 days to clear invoices.
Late Payment Clause
Specify 1.5% monthly interest on past-due balances to discourage payment holds.

Best Practices

5 Proven Tips to Get Paid on Time

Chasing overdue payments hurts business momentum. Adopt these 5 proven tactics to get paid faster:

1
Always Request an Upfront Deposit: For projects over $1,000, secure a 30% to 50% retainer before starting work.
2
Invoice Same-Day: Issue bills immediately upon milestone completion while client appreciation is fresh.
3
Provide Multi-Channel Rails: Include direct bank ACH/wire details, routing codes, or a payment link.
4
Automate Polite Check-Ins: Send friendly payment reminders 3 days before and on the due date.
5
Avoid Vague Line Descriptions: Clear item descriptions prevent client disputes and accounting hold-ups.
